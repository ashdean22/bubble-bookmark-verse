# Refine photorealistic soap bubbles

## Scope
- Change only the bubble surface styling and canvas background.
- Preserve bubble movement, collisions, dragging, menus, sizing, icons, pricing, storage, and all other behavior.

## Visual treatment
- Use a deep neutral background so transparent film remains visible.
- Keep bubble centers mostly clear with subtle refraction rather than a cloudy fill.
- Strengthen irregular edge interference in blue, green, red, purple, pink, and cyan.
- Add asymmetric glossy reflections, a fine illuminated rim, and restrained optical depth for a photographic soap-film appearance.
- Keep icons legible and avoid a plastic or cartoon-like finish.

## Verification
- Inspect populated desktop and phone-sized views.
- Confirm bubbles remain visible, controls still respond, and movement is unchanged.
- Confirm the project builds without errors.
