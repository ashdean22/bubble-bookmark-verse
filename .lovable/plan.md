# Refine bubble realism

## Scope
- Change only the soap-bubble surface styling.
- Preserve movement, collision physics, sizing, interactions, layout, pricing, storage, and all other behavior.

## Visual treatment
- Make the center clearer and less milky.
- Concentrate thin-film rainbow color into irregular, narrow rim sections rather than a full saturated ring.
- Add asymmetric window highlights and a subtle curved environmental reflection band based on the reference photo.
- Refine the edge into a crisp illuminated lip with a softly shaded lower hemisphere.
- Keep site icons readable without making the bubble resemble solid plastic.

## Verification
- Inspect the result on desktop and phone-sized views.
- Confirm bubble movement and touch controls remain unchanged.
- Confirm the project builds without errors.
